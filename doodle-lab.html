A · line doodles

Even though I came in with some knowledge of the beauty industry, I couldn’t assume I understood everyone’s needs.

  • I drew from my experience working in the industry to turn problems I’d noticed into something tangible.
  • Talking with people about their experiences and frustrations showed me needs I hadn’t considered.
  • Seeing where people struggled during testing helped me challenge my initial assumptions and make more thoughtful design decisions.

Skincare is something so many people around me care about, so getting to have deeper conversations with them was one of my favorite parts of this project.
