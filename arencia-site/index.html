Summary

During my internship at Arencia, I worked with the marketing team on rebranding the brand’s Instagram to strengthen its visual identity in the U.S. market. After researching the brand and its competitors, I made a creative direction deck built around three brand values, artisanal, natural and scientific, and worked with a filming team to turn it into a 12-post grid and a short-form video.

Overview

Context

Introducing Arencia to U.S. shoppers

As Arencia prepared to expand into U.S. retailers through the Sephora x Olive Young collaboration, the brand wanted a stronger presence among U.S. consumers.

Looking at past campaigns, I found three problems to solve: the feed was hard to recognize as one brand, it didn’t connect with U.S. audiences, and it didn’t say much about what makes Arencia different.

Problem 1

Weak brand recognition

The feed lacked a consistent, recognizable visual identity.

Problem 2

Low U.S. engagement

Past posts didn’t connect or perform as well with U.S. audiences.

Problem 3

Unclear brand story

Posts didn’t clearly communicate Arencia’s brand values.

Goal

How might we create a social media campaign that builds a stronger, more recognizable brand presence for Arencia among U.S. consumers?

My role

From research to a creative direction deck to the final shoot

I worked with Arencia’s marketing team and led the creative direction of the rebrand. The work moved through four stages.

01 Research

Find what to fix

Reviewed past campaigns to pin down the three problems, named the brand values with the team, and analyzed competitor campaigns.

02 Creative direction

Make the deck

Turned each brand value into a visual direction for backgrounds, props, framing and lighting, plus the passport concept for the video, all in one deck.

03 Handoff

Brief the filming team

The deck went to the filming team as the brief for every photo and video shoot.

04 Production

Create the final content

Worked with the filming team to turn the directions into the final 12-post grid and short-form video.

Competitor campaign analysis

How other brands show the same values

I analyzed past campaigns from beauty brands that share Arencia’s values, breaking down the visual cues that make each one feel scientific or artisanal.

Dr. Althea, 345 Line campaign

Scientific feel

  • Silver, metallic tones
  • Cool, clinical lighting
  • White, neutral backgrounds
  • Minimal compositions
  • Glossy, reflective surfaces

Aesop, Aurner Eau de Parfum campaign

Artisanal feel

  • Dramatic lighting
  • Paper or tactile textures
  • Hand-drawn elements
  • Warm, earthy tones
  • Visible hands, human touch

Insight

Borrowing from both, in Arencia’s own colors

Arencia’s values span both looks, so instead of copying one brand I took the cues that carried each feeling and gave them a place in the grid.

What I saw

Science reads as silver and glass

Dr. Althea signals science with metallic tones, glossy surfaces and clean, minimal frames.

What I decided

Metal and magnification

Silver tubes and trays, metal tools, close-ups and glass distortion in the scientific and natural posts.

What I saw

Craft reads as touch

Aesop signals craft with tactile paper, hand-drawn marks, warm tones and visible hands.

What I decided

Paper, brushstrokes and hands

Textured traditional paper and brush calligraphy in the artisanal posts, and a hand-stitched passport stamped by hand in the video.

What I saw

Two looks risk two brands

Mixing both styles could repeat the old feed’s problem: posts that don’t read as one brand.

What I decided

One palette across every post

Olive green, gold and soft white tie all twelve posts together, whichever value they show.

In the creative direction deck, I set a visual direction for each brand value. The filming team shot every post from it, and together we combined the posts into one cohesive 12-post grid. Olive green, gold and soft white tie them together.

Video

Short-form video

A passport for your skin

With the filming team, I made a short video from the deck’s passport-control travel concept, standing for Arencia’s arrival in the U.S. Each passport belongs to a different person with a different skin concern, and gets stamped with the Booster Shot that targets it.

Textured paper, a hand-stitched passport and handwritten names keep it artisanal and personal. Varied angles and zooms add movement, and the act of stamping makes it feel hands-on.

  1. A passport is received and opened to reveal a skin concern.

  2. The matching Booster Shot is stamped, and the passport is returned.

  3. The sequence repeats with different names, skin concerns and Booster Shots.

  4. A blank page is stamped with the Arencia logo to end the video.
