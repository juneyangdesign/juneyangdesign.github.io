Summary

During my internship at Arencia, I worked with the marketing team on rebranding the brand’s Instagram to strengthen its visual identity in the U.S. market. I art directed a 12-post grid and a short-form video built around three brand values: artisanal, natural and scientific.

Overview Research Post grid Video Impact

Overview

Context

Introducing Arencia to U.S. shoppers

As Arencia prepared to expand into U.S. retailers through the Sephora x Olive Young collaboration, the brand wanted a stronger presence among U.S. consumers.

Looking at past campaigns, I found three problems to solve: the feed was hard to recognize as one brand, it didn’t connect with U.S. audiences, and it didn’t say much about what makes Arencia different.

Problem 1

Weak brand recognition

The feed lacked a consistent, recognizable visual identity.

Problem 2

Low U.S. engagement

Past posts didn’t connect or perform as well with U.S. audiences.

Problem 3

Unclear brand story

Posts didn’t clearly communicate Arencia’s brand values.

Goal

How might we create a social media campaign that builds a stronger, more recognizable brand presence for Arencia among U.S. consumers?

Research

Brand story

Three values to show in every post

I worked with the marketing team to name three key brand values for the campaign to communicate.

01

Artisanal

Time-intensive processes rooted in Korean tradition.

02

Natural

Natural ingredients, and processes that keep their benefits.

03

Scientific

Modern skincare research behind effective formulas.

Competitor campaign analysis

How other brands show the same values

I looked at past campaigns from beauty brands with similar values to see how they communicated them visually.

Post grid

12-post grid

Three visual directions, one grid

I created a visual direction for each brand value and combined them into a cohesive 12-post grid. Olive green, gold and soft white tie the posts together.

Every shoot followed the direction below, from backgrounds and props to framing and lighting. See the posts on @arencia_global.

Artisanal

Product

  • Dark green, textured backgrounds
  • Textured traditional paper
  • Close-up framing

Calligraphy

  • Loose, handwritten brushstrokes
  • Textured traditional paper

Natural

Model

  • Minimal styling
  • Relaxed poses and expressions
  • Soft, diffused light that brings out the skin’s glow

Ingredient

  • Fresh green ingredients, arranged neatly
  • A metallic silver tray for a scientific touch

Scientific

Workspace

  • Organized, but not too polished

Lifestyle and product

  • Products, metal tools and everyday items spilling out of a pouch
  • Close-ups with glass distortion and magnifying effects

Video

Short-form video

A passport for your skin

A short video built on a passport-control travel concept, standing for Arencia’s arrival in the U.S. Each passport belongs to a different person with a different skin concern, and gets stamped with the Booster Shot that targets it.

Textured paper, a hand-stitched passport and handwritten names keep it artisanal and personal. Varied angles and zooms add movement, and the act of stamping makes it feel hands-on.

  1. A passport is received and opened to reveal a skin concern.

  2. The matching Booster Shot is stamped, and the passport is returned.

  3. The sequence repeats with different names, skin concerns and Booster Shots.

  4. A blank page is stamped with the Arencia logo to end the video.
