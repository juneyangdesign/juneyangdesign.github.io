Summary

During my internship at Arencia, I worked with the marketing team to rebrand the brand’s Instagram and strengthen its visual identity in the U.S. market. I researched past campaigns and competitors, then developed a creative direction deck around three key brand values: artisanal, natural, and scientific. I collaborated with the filming team on three shoots, each focused on a different brand value, to create a cohesive 12-post Instagram grid and a short-form video.

Overview

Context

Introducing Arencia to U.S. shoppers

As Arencia prepared to expand into U.S. retailers through the Sephora x Olive Young partnership, the brand wanted to build a stronger presence among U.S. consumers.

After reviewing past campaigns, I identified three main problems with the brand’s existing Instagram content.

Problem 1

Weak brand recognition

The feed lacked a consistent, recognizable visual identity.

Problem 2

Low U.S. engagement

Past posts didn’t connect or perform as well with U.S. audiences.

Problem 3

Unclear brand story

Posts didn’t clearly communicate Arencia’s brand values.

Goal

How might we create a social media campaign that builds a stronger, more recognizable brand presence for Arencia among U.S. consumers?

Competitor campaign analysis

How other brands communicate similar values

I analyzed campaigns from beauty brands with similar values to Arencia, focusing on how they used color, lighting, textures, and composition to communicate those values visually.

Dr. Althea — 345 Line campaign

Scientific aesthetic

  • Silver and metallic tones
  • Cool, clinical lighting
  • Clean, neutral backgrounds
  • Minimal compositions
  • Glossy, reflective surfaces

Aesop — Aurner Eau de Parfum campaign

Artisanal aesthetic

  • Dramatic lighting
  • Tactile paper textures
  • Hand-drawn elements
  • Warm, earthy tones
  • Visible hands and human touch

Insights

Combining both styles into one cohesive identity

I noticed that scientific and artisanal brands often use very different visual styles. Since Arencia combines both values, I wanted to bring elements from each into the campaign while keeping the overall feed consistent.

What I noticed

Visual elements that create a scientific feel

Brands like Dr. Althea use metallic tones, reflective surfaces, and minimal compositions to create a clean, scientific look.

How I applied it

Metallic props and magnification

I incorporated silver tubes, metal trays, close-up photography, and glass distortion into the scientific and natural posts.

What I noticed

Craft is communicated through texture and human touch

Brands like Aesop use textured materials, hand-drawn details, warm tones, and visible hands to emphasize craftsmanship.

How I applied it

Traditional paper and handmade details

I incorporated traditional paper textures and brush calligraphy into the artisanal posts, along with a hand-stitched passport and hand-stamped details for the video.

What I noticed

Different styles can make the feed feel disconnected

Combining scientific and artisanal elements without a consistent visual direction could make the posts feel like they belong to different brands.

How I applied it

A consistent color palette

I used olive green, gold, and soft white across all 12 posts to connect the different visual styles and create a more cohesive feed.

Video

Short-form video

A passport for your skin

I developed a passport-control concept to represent Arencia’s arrival in the U.S. and worked with the filming team to bring it to life. Each passport represents someone with a different skin concern and gets stamped with the Booster Shot designed to address it.

For the creative direction, I proposed using textured paper, hand-stitched passports, and handwritten names to give the video a personal, handmade feel. I also suggested varied camera angles and close-up shots to add movement, with the stamping action as the main focus.

  1. A passport is received and opened to reveal a skin concern.

  2. The matching Booster Shot is stamped onto the page, and the passport is returned.

  3. The process repeats with different names, skin concerns, and Booster Shots.

  4. The video ends with the Arencia logo stamped onto a blank page.

Reflection

What I learned

01

Turning research into creative decisions

This project taught me how to use research to make more intentional creative decisions. Instead of just focusing on what looked good, I learned to think about why certain visual elements worked and how they could help communicate Arencia’s brand values. It was interesting to see how small decisions about color, lighting, and composition could shape the overall brand identity.

02

Communicating ideas clearly

Working with the filming team taught me how important it is to communicate creative ideas clearly. I learned to make my directions more specific so the team could understand the look and feel I had in mind. It was rewarding to see the concepts I developed in the deck turn into actual photos and videos, while also learning to stay flexible and open to feedback during production.

03

Designing with a larger goal in mind

I also learned to think beyond individual pieces of content and consider how they contribute to a larger marketing goal. This project helped me understand that creating a strong visual identity isn’t just about making posts look consistent. It’s also about communicating what makes a brand unique and connecting with the audience it’s trying to reach.
