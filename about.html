Work About Connect

Hi, I’m June!

I love creating things that bring joy to people :)

I’m interested in how design can solve real problems and make everyday experiences better. I especially enjoy putting myself in other people’s shoes, understanding what they need, and coming up with creative ways to make things easier and more enjoyable.

I’m currently a junior at Carnegie Mellon University studying Communication Design and Human-Computer Interaction. I’ve had the chance to explore my interests through projects in UX design, product design, and brand marketing. I love that each gives me a different way to think about problems and create something that people can connect with.

Outside of school and design projects, I still love creating things just for fun, whether that’s a carefully curated Spotify playlist or Pinterest board. I also love exploring new cafés and bakeries for a sweet treat and spending time with my amazing friends!
